VeriRAG Evaluation Sample Policy

Retention
Uploaded research documents are stored locally and kept until the researcher deletes them.

Privacy
Questions sent to a configured external language model may include retrieved passages. The sample policy does not state whether local files are encrypted at rest.

Conflicting draft note
A later draft note says records are removed after thirty days. The note does not explain whether it replaces the retention section.

This small file is the source text for the sample evaluation PDF. The evaluation questions include answerable, partially answerable, unanswerable, and conflicting-evidence cases. The expected labels are intentionally blank until claims are annotated under a documented policy.
